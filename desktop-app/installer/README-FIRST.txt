DESKTOPCRAFT DESKTOP EDITION
============================

Desktopcraft includes the complete five-course learning studio, 2,500 lessons,
quizzes, simulator, tutorials, App Maker, lesson creator, Helper AI course guide,
builder forum, accounts, progress, leaderboard, and customization controls.

Java 17 or newer is required.

WINDOWS
1. Double-click Install-Desktopcraft-Windows.bat.
2. The app and a launcher will be placed on your Desktop.
3. Double-click Launch Desktopcraft.bat on your Desktop.

LINUX
1. Open a terminal in this folder.
2. Run: chmod +x install-desktopcraft.sh
3. Run: ./install-desktopcraft.sh
4. Open Desktopcraft from the launcher placed on your Desktop.

macOS
1. Double-click Install Desktopcraft.command. If macOS blocks it, right-click it
   and choose Open, or run: chmod +x "Install Desktopcraft.command"
2. The app and Launch Desktopcraft.command will be placed on your Desktop.

You can also run the app directly:

    java -jar Desktopcraft.jar

The ZIP cannot choose where your unzip program extracts files. The included
installer copies Desktopcraft to your Desktop immediately after you run it.
